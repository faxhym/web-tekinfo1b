# Product context

A community/student contribution to faxhym/web-tekinfo1b, not an official replacement for the institution's website. Audience: prospective students browsing on phones and desktops. Purpose: introduce the TI department and guide visitors to official program information.

Approved scope: full visual redesign in plain HTML/CSS/JavaScript; preserve institutional identity and relevant source assets, improve responsive behavior, accessibility, performance, documentation, and tests. Work locally only; no push, PR, or deployment without additional approval. User delegated visual and implementation decisions.

Content authority: original repository and linked official ti.pnp.ac.id pages. Four programs and existing accreditation values are historical source content, not freshly verified facts; label their provenance and direct users to official information. Never invent statistics, facilities, deadlines, admissions links, or accreditation validity dates. No backend, tracking, forms, or framework. Preserve upstream license status (no license added).

Builder decisions: readable daylight-friendly light surfaces with blue institutional identity, deliberate type hierarchy, a program explorer that remains usable without JS, optional rather than autoplay video, locally served assets. No external font/runtime dependency.
